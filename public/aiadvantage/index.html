The economics changed

The old way is expensive

How many people does it take to launch one good idea?

Traditionally, a serious launch could require one person for branding, another for copy, another for the website, another for graphics, funnels, content, ads, video, or development. Every new idea meant another meeting, another handoff, and another invoice.

That model made sense when specialized labor was the only practical way to produce the work. But AI has changed the economics.

Before spending thousands, ask a better question: “Do I actually need to hire this out—or can AI help me do it?”

Some businesses barely use AI. Others are quietly building workflows that let small teams produce an extraordinary amount of work. Eventually, using AI well may feel as normal as using search, email, or a smartphone.

Learn while the skill still creates separation—not after it becomes table stakes.

Questions people ask before they start

“But what if…”

I’m not technical.

You do not need to become a programmer. You need to learn how to give AI useful context, direction, examples, constraints, and feedback so it can help accomplish real business goals.

I already tried ChatGPT and the results were generic.

Generic prompts usually create generic work. Better context, structure, examples, iteration, and judgment can dramatically improve the result. The skill is not simply “using ChatGPT.” It is learning to direct it.

I already have a marketing person.

Great. This can make a good marketer more capable. The goal is not automatic replacement; it is getting more output, faster experimentation, and better leverage from the people and resources you already have.

I run an agency. Isn't AI bad for agencies?

It can be an advantage. Agencies that increase speed, variation, capacity, and turnaround without proportionally increasing payroll can become more competitive—while keeping human strategy and judgment where they matter.

I don't have time to learn another tool.

The goal is not to add another hobby. It is to reduce the time and friction involved in work already on your plate. Start with the bottleneck that costs you the most time or money.

Won't everything look AI-generated?

Only if you use AI generically. Your experience, values, customer knowledge, strategy, taste, brand voice, and human judgment remain the differentiator.
